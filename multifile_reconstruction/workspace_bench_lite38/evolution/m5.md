# Allocate monthly payroll across effective departments

Add visible `PayrollCoverage` and `PayrollCosts` input sheets. In `PayrollCoverage`, the first column header is `Month`, and the rows list months for which the individual payroll is complete. In `PayrollCosts`, the first three headers are `Employee ID`, `Month`, and `Salary`. A month is the Excel date of its first day. Support at least 30 payroll records. Inputs use one record per employee per month and a nonnegative amount with at most two decimal places. Leave the inputs blank in the delivered workbook.

For a covered month, allocate each employee's salary across only that employee's employment days. Use the existing rule that a day is eligible strictly after hire and strictly before separation. Include all employment periods for the same person, excluding gaps. For each eligible day, use the department reported under the existing permanent transfer, temporary assignment and organization succession rules on that day. Convert salary to cents, assign the integer quotient to all eligible days, and assign the remainder one cent at a time from the earliest eligible days. A salary record with no eligible day is unallocated. A payroll record excluded from the covered month must not enter that month's amount.

Use the existing `HRCostAnalysis` month selector and reporting year from `MonthlyChangeQuery`. Add the visible workbook-level names `AllocatedSalary` and `AllocatedEmployeeDays`, each a contiguous seven-cell range in `MonthlyChangeQuery!C5:I5` department order, and `UnallocatedSalary`, a single cell. These show salary amounts and eligible days for the selected covered month. Update the existing `HRCostAnalysis!C11` salary total to the sum actually allocated for that month. The existing total must then include that salary and the original non-salary categories for the selected month. Outside covered periods, keep the existing monthly cost report behavior and leave the new allocations at zero. Preserve prior reports and input interfaces, including the original records. Return the recalculating XLSX.
