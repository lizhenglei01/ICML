# Handle effective-dated department mergers and closures

Upgrade the supplied HR workbook to report historical and current organization structures using the existing department codes. Add a visible `DepartmentChanges` ledger with `Effective Date`, `Source Department`, and `Successor Department` as its first three columns. A blank successor means a closure. Use rows starting at row 2 and support at least 30 events in chronological order. The input uses existing department names as keys. Tested mergers are acyclic and can be chained. Tested closures without a successor are of unoccupied departments with no ongoing assignment there. Leave the ledger blank on delivery.

An organization change applies from its effective date inclusive. Before that date, the original department continues to apply. At or after that date, report people in the successor department, following any later effective mergers. An ongoing temporary assignment follows its host department's succession. When the temporary assignment ends, return to the employee's then-current permanent department and apply its succession. A valid permanent transfer after a merger must name the effective legal departments at that transfer date. A new temporary assignment starting on or after a department has closed cannot use that department as host. A new permanent transfer with a retired department as its origin or destination cannot take effect. Existing events that started before a merger still apply through their successor; keep their original data intact. Rejected events must have no impact on occupation or history.

Update the current department lookup, current department summary, monthly and annual department headcounts, and monthly and annual employment activity. A hire or separation is attributed to the employment period's starting department as mapped on the event date. A rehire follows the same rule. Changing a department counts as neither a hire nor a separation. Keep all seven existing report labels, including labels retired in a later period. A retired department has zero occupancy after retirement. Preserve the existing inputs, output names and scope of M1–M3. Keep monthly salary and total cost, contract reminders and original input records intact. Deliver an XLSX that recalculates for new valid inputs.
